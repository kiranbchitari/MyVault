Android app

MyVault

An offline, encrypted vault for your passwords, cards and documents.

Keep passwords, PINs, bank details, cards, Wi-Fi networks, identity documents and notes in one place. Everything is encrypted on your phone, with no account and no servers.

See Features Privacy Policy
Features

Your secrets, locked on your phone.

🔐

Encrypted vault

Everything is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM, unlocked by your master passphrase.

👆

Biometric unlock

Optional fingerprint unlock, plus auto-lock when you leave the app or the screen turns off.

🗂️

Ten record types

Passwords, PINs, bank accounts, cards, Wi-Fi, identity documents, developer secrets and notes.

🔎

Private search

Search titles, tags and notes in memory. Secret values are never searched or indexed.

🎲

Password generator

Strong random passwords with the length and character types you choose.

☁️

Encrypted backups

Save encrypted .vault backups anywhere, or to your own Google Drive.

Privacy

Only you hold the key.

No account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Optional Google Drive backup uploads only encrypted files to a MyVault Backups folder in your own Drive.

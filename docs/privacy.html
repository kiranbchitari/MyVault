Privacy Policy for MyVault

MyVault is an offline, encrypted vault for Android. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: your data is encrypted on your phone and, if you choose, backed up encrypted to your own Google Drive. The developer never receives it and cannot read it.

Data you enter

Passwords, PINs, bank details, card details, Wi-Fi networks, identity documents and their images, notes, tags and settings you add are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The keys are protected by your master passphrase (and, if you turn it on, your fingerprint through Android's Keystore). MyVault has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Your master passphrase is never stored or sent anywhere. If you forget it and have no backup with a known passphrase, the developer cannot recover your data.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you tap Connect Google Drive, the app asks you to sign in with Google and grant the drive.file permission. With it, MyVault can only see and manage files it created itself. It cannot read any other file in your Drive.

  • Backups are .vault files, encrypted on your phone before upload, saved in a MyVault Backups folder in your own Google Drive. They count against your own Drive storage. MyVault keeps the newest 10 and removes older ones it created.
  • Neither Google nor the developer can read the contents of these backups without your passphrase.
  • Your phone uploads and downloads these files directly with Google over HTTPS. Nothing passes through, or is stored on, any server run by the developer.
  • The app uses the access token Google issues only to read and write those backup files, does not store the token, and never sees your Google password.

MyVault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's handling of data in your Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Backup files you save

When you save an encrypted backup or recovery kit to a location you choose, what happens to that file afterwards (for example, sharing it) is up to you.

Android device backup

MyVault opts out of Android's own cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your vault is not copied by those services.

Other protections

  • The vault locks automatically when you leave the app, when the screen turns off, or after a period of inactivity.
  • Screenshots, screen recording and the Recents preview are blocked.
  • Copied secrets are cleared from the clipboard after a short time.

Your control

  • Delete any item in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • Delete Drive backups by removing the MyVault Backups folder or its files in Google Drive.
  • Revoke MyVault's Drive access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

MyVault is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
